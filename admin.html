Marketing

Home page banner

The big photo with a headline at the top of the shop. Add several photos and they fade from one to the next behind the headline.

Announcement bar

A short line across the top of the shop, e.g. “11.11 Sale · 15% off everything with code QI1111”.

Singapore self-collect

Switch on when you are going to Singapore, so Singapore customers can choose to collect from you instead of DHL (RM88). Switch off when you are back.

Collections

Groups of pieces shown as tiles on the shop's home page and in the Shop menu, e.g. “Rare Finds” or “Gifts under RM100”.

Coupon codes

Customers type the code at checkout. Share it on Instagram, WhatsApp or with VIP customers.

Affiliates

Friends who help sell. Each gets a code and a link. Website orders through them count automatically; for bazaar or WhatsApp sales, choose the seller when you record Sold offline on the Stock page.

Sales

Automatic % off, no code needed. The shop shows the old price crossed out and a “Sale” label.
