Crystal care

  • Remove your crystals before showering, swimming or exercise, and keep them away from perfume and chemicals.
  • Rose quartz, amethyst and other coloured stones can fade in strong direct sunlight. Cleanse them under moonlight, with sound, or on a selenite plate instead.
  • Bracelets are strung on elastic cord. Roll them on and off rather than stretching them.
  • Store each piece separately in its pouch.
