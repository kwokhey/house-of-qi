Online payment on this website is for delivery within Malaysia and Singapore. For other countries, please contact our customer service on WhatsApp before ordering. We will confirm availability, the shipping cost, the expected delivery time and how to pay.

Import duties, taxes and customs charges in your country are not included and are paid by the customer. Delivery times depend on customs clearance and the courier in your country.

Unworn pieces in their original packaging may be returned or exchanged within 14 days of delivery. Contact us to start a return. Pieces made to a custom size or engraved cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged.

Every crystal is natural, so colour, inclusions and pattern vary slightly from the photos. This is not a defect.

Crystal care

  • Remove your crystals before showering, swimming or exercise, and keep them away from perfume and chemicals.
  • Rose quartz, amethyst and other coloured stones can fade in strong direct sunlight. Cleanse them under moonlight, with sound, or on a selenite plate instead.
  • Bracelets are strung on elastic cord. Roll them on and off rather than stretching them.
  • Store each piece separately in its pouch.
