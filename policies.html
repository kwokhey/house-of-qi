Returns & Exchanges

Unworn pieces in their original packaging may be returned or exchanged within 14 days of delivery. Email us to begin a return.

Crystal Care

Remove your crystals before showering, swimming or exercise, and keep them away from perfume and chemicals. Store each piece separately in its pouch. Rose quartz, amethyst and other coloured stones can fade in strong direct sunlight — cleanse them under moonlight or with sound instead. Crystal bracelets are strung on elastic cord; roll them on and off rather than stretching.

Privacy
