Our Promise

Real crystals, honestly chosen

Dyed stones and glass imitations are everywhere. Here's what makes a natural crystal real.

01

Natural inclusions

Tiny clouds, lines and "imperfections" are nature's fingerprint — not flaws.

02

Natural colour variation

Real stones show gentle shifts in tone. Perfectly uniform colour is often a sign of dye.

03

Cool to the touch

Genuine crystal feels cool and has a satisfying weight in the hand.
